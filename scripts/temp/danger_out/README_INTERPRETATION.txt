DANGER-ZONE ANALYSIS — interpretation (read before using)
=========================================================
Ran on the real 10-model × 3-romanized-tier judge labels (5500 items).

RESULT: a nominal inverted-U — harm-aware compliance peaks at intermediate
comprehension (~0.54), bootstrap 925/1000 concave.

BUT THIS DOES NOT SURVIVE SCRUTINY (verified by tier split):
  - Arabizi only (cleanest, naturalistic): NOT concave (417/1000) — no danger zone.
  - ITRANS only (least reliable tier): strong (916/1000) — drives the whole effect.
  - Magnitudes are tiny (harm-compliance mostly 0-12%); the concavity is partly
    structural (y bounded near 0 at both comprehension extremes).
  - The ITRANS tier is exactly the one the paper flags as least reliable
    (native Hindi poorly comprehended; human judge kappa only 0.59-0.62).

CONCLUSION: the "danger zone" is an ITRANS artifact, NOT a robust discovery.
Do NOT promote it to a central claim. At most a heavily-caveated exploratory note.
The main-track lever remains the direction-transfer experiment, not this.
